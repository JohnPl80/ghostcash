# Aetherians background music

Original tracks made by the owner's own local AI ("Skynet") for this channel,
owned by the owner (stated 2026-11-24). Generated locally with ACE-Step
(1.5 turbo, ComfyUI; fallback ACE-Step v1 3.5B), an Apache-2.0 licensed
model, from original style prompts (no named songs or artists imitated).

Used by `video.music_files` in config/brands/aetherians.yaml; each video gets
one, chosen by its content id. GhostCash levels them (they range from -12.7 to
-27.5 LUFS as delivered), lines up each track's ending with the end of the
video and fades both edges.
