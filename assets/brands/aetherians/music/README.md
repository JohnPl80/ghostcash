# Aetherians background music

Original tracks made by the owner's own local AI ("Skynet") for this channel,
owned by the owner (stated 2026-11-24). Used by `video.music_files` in
config/brands/aetherians.yaml; each video gets one, chosen by its content id.
GhostCash levels them (they range from -12.7 to -27.5 LUFS as delivered),
lines up each track's ending with the end of the video and fades both edges.
